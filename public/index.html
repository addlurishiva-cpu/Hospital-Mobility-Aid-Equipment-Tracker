🏥

Clinical Objective

Eliminates lost mobility aids and prevents hospital-acquired cross-infections by guaranteeing that reusable equipment (wheelchairs, hoists, infusion pumps, dynamic mattresses) is accounted for at every ward transition and certified sterile before reuse.

🧼

Infection Control Gate

Equipment returned from patient care is automatically routed into DIRTY_PENDING_CLEANING. It is physically quarantined in the soiled bay and cannot be re-issued until a decontamination specialist verifies the 3-point inspection and enters the chemical lot batch.

🛠️

Biomedical Safety Blackout

Any staff member can flag mechanical damage or calibration errors, triggering an immediate MAINTENANCE_BLACKOUT lock. Equipment is withdrawn to the Biomedical Depot and requires Electrical Safety Testing (EST) sign-off prior to clearance.

🛡️

Least Privilege RBAC

Enforced across 4 distinct role tiers: Visitor (read-only public metrics), Requester (nurse check-out and ward requests), Operator (logistics porters and cleaning tech), and Administrator (asset configuration & audit controls).
